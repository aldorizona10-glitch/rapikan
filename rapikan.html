How it works

01

Read, don't guess

An LLM extracts each field and copies a verbatim quote from your text as evidence. Values it had to infer are marked, not hidden.

02

Flag what's missing

Anything ambiguous or absent — no delivery address, no stated total — is raised for a human instead of being invented.

03

You approve

Drafted replies and structured JSON are yours to edit and copy. The tool never sends, saves to a server, or acts on its own.
